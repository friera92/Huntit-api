## J. W. Corbett facts to review

- Hog: archery August 29–September 20, 2026; muzzleloading October 3–18; general gun November 7–January 3, 2027; small game January 4–February 21, 2027. No size/bag limit and no hunting license for hog; management-area and applicable season permits still apply unless exempt. No quota permit. Hog dogs only during general gun, outside still-hunt areas, with tracking/correction requirements.
- Turkey: youth February 26–March 1, 2027; spring March 6–April 11 **Wednesdays, Saturdays and Sundays only**. Only Florida residents may hunt turkey March 6–14. No fall turkey season is listed. Gobbler or bearded turkey only; area daily limit 1, with statewide spring season/possession limits 2. Youth must be under 16 and directly supervised by an adult 18+; adults may assist but cannot harvest during youth turkey.
- Turkey methods: bows, crossbows, PCP bolt/arrow airguns and centerfire/muzzleloading shotguns with No. 2 or smaller shot. Shooting ends at sunset. Hunting license, management-area permit and turkey permit unless exempt; annual hunting license for nonresidents. No quota permit.
- Bruce Bowman is bows only, subject to the stated disability permit exceptions; rifle buffer restrictions apply elsewhere. Check-station and harvest reporting obligations are retained. Off-road hunting vehicle use requires the new OHV hunting permit from July 31, 2026; it does not authorize otherwise prohibited vehicle types. The August 16–28 closure has limited road/trail exceptions in the brochure.
- Python: non-firearm removal year-round, lawful firearms only during open brochure hunting seasons using season-allowed methods; no live removal. Bruce Bowman permits hand tools/bows with the specified disability exceptions. Dogs may search only during general gun. The spring turkey section prohibits hunting other animals; the draft does not interpret this as blanket firearm permission for reptiles and directs confirmation of that interaction with FWC.

## Holey Land facts to review

- Deer and hog: archery August 8–30, 2026; muzzleloading September 5–13; general gun-walk October 3–18; general gun-vehicle November 21–December 6. The December 7–March 7 small-game list **does not include wild hog or deer**.
- Deer: antlered only, with one antler at least 5 inches and at least two points of at least 1 inch; statewide youth antler-point exception retained. Area season limit 1 during archery/muzzleloading/general gun-walk; general gun-vehicle limit 1 per antlered deer tag. Statewide annual/daily/possession limits also apply.
- General gun-vehicle requires its quota/track permit and supplied antlered deer tag; quota exemptions and guest hunters are not allowed. Walk-hunting is closed and motorized access is limited to authorized vehicles. Deer dogs need the WMA deer-dog permit; tracking/correction devices are required. Other listed deer/hog seasons do not require quota permits.
- Hog has no hunting-license, bag or size requirement; management-area/season/quota requirements still apply. Turkey take is expressly prohibited throughout the brochure year; no fabricated open turkey season is created.
- Entry is through the L-5 or Miami Canal levees; levees/roads/ramps/parking/camping areas and adjacent powerline pads are closed to hunting. Weekend deer check-station and immediate vehicle-hunt tag requirements remain explicit.
- Python: year-round non-firearm opportunity, season-limited firearm use, no live removal; centerfire rifle take prohibited March 8–April 11, 2027. Dogs may only search during general gun-vehicle season. Vehicle and closure restrictions still apply.

## Rotenberger facts to review

- Deer and hog: archery August 8–30, 2026; muzzleloading September 5–13; general gun-walk October 3–18. There is **no listed 2026–2027 general gun-vehicle hunt**. Access prose mentioning that season does not create one. The December 7–March 7 small-game list does not include hog/deer.
- Deer antlers and statewide limits follow the two-point/five-inch criteria and youth exception described above. Area season limit 1 antlered deer during the listed deer seasons. No quota permit for these hunts.
- Hog has no hunting-license, bag or size requirement; applicable management-area/season permits remain. Dogs cannot hunt deer/hog. Turkey take is expressly prohibited.
- Access is through the L-4, Powerline, Miami Canal and Manley Ditch levees. Hunting on/from/across levees and in other listed access facilities is prohibited. Airboats are prohibited during the three deer/hog hunts; other vehicle/scouting/flag restrictions remain in the brochure.
- Python: year-round non-firearm opportunity, season-limited firearm use, no live removal and no dogs. Centerfire rifle take is prohibited after small game ends on March 7, 2027.

## Shared supporting sources and boundaries

[FWC hunting licenses and permits](https://myfwc.com/license/recreational/hunting/), [FWC taking-game equipment rules](https://myfwc.com/hunting/regulations/taking-game/), [FWC python removal](https://myfwc.com/wildlifehabitats/nonnatives/python/removing/) and [FWC humane killing guidance](https://myfwc.com/wildlifehabitats/nonnatives/python/humane-killing-methods/) support the linked draft requirements. The equipment source adds 7/8-inch minimum broadheads for deer/turkey, species-specific PCP minimums, and deer muzzleloader minimums. These do not authorize weapons prohibited by a particular area/season; Corbett spring turkey still prohibits PCP bullets. Statutory exemptions do not override quota rules marked no exemptions, required tags, area closures or method restrictions. The summaries do not claim to reproduce every provision of the full brochure.

The scope is four existing species and selected existing catalog areas, not every Florida WMA. Review coverage ends June 30, 2027; this is a maintenance boundary. Current closure notices and subsequently revised official rules must still be checked.
