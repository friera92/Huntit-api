## Hog and python review

The [FWC wild-hog page](https://myfwc.com/hunting/wild-hog/) supports the private-land exception. Check the written-permission and identification/tracking/correction requirements when dogs are used, lawful methods, and the separate FDACS authorization for live transport/holding. This private-land exception is never applied to a WMA search.

The [FWC python removal page](https://myfwc.com/wildlifehabitats/nonnatives/python/removing/) supplies the 32-area list and its year-round no-license/no-permit/no-bag-limit scope. The [Burmese-python regulatory-status page](https://myfwc.com/wildlifehabitats/profiles/reptiles/snakes/burmese-python/) supplies anti-cruelty and permission context. [FWC humane-removal guidance](https://myfwc.com/wildlifehabitats/nonnatives/python/humane-killing-methods/) supplies humane onsite removal and the restriction on public live transport. Local firearm rules, closures and area access provisions remain applicable. The exact 32 names are frozen in the approved release and shown in the admin review summary; they were compared against the current official list. `backend/integration/starter.py` supplies working copies for future maintenance, not approval authority.

The python method summary identifies the tools described in FWC's humane-method guidance: captive-bolt equipment or a lawfully used firearm/PCP air gun to produce immediate unconsciousness, followed by the required second step (pithing) to prevent recovery. It links the full FWC/AVMA guidance, calls for appropriate training and retains property-specific firearm restrictions. The summary is non-graphic and does not treat the first step alone as sufficient.

## Manual administrator review for future changes

1. Complete local setup, then create your own administrator: `.venv\Scripts\python.exe backend/manage.py createsuperuser`. No default administrator credentials are supplied.
2. Start the app and open `http://127.0.0.1:8000/admin/`. Open **Integration → Regulation drafts**. A designated reviewer needs staff status plus the **Can review and publish regulatory snapshots** permission, together with normal draft view/change permissions. A superuser has those permissions.
3. Open each applicable draft. The form displays the source links and a readable rule summary above the structured working copy. Compare dates, land/area/DMU scope, limits, permits, methods and exceptions against the current sources. Correct the working copy and its source-checked date when needed. **Save** alone does not publish those edits; any previously approved snapshot stays unchanged.
4. Check the statement that you personally checked the sources and enter meaningful review notes. Click **Publish reviewed revision**. This creates the immutable public snapshot and timestamps verification for its sources and matched results.
5. Test a guest search at an opening date, a closing date and an excluded date/area. Confirm the displayed sources and verification date. Repeat for every supported draft you intend to publish.
6. For changes, edit the draft and perform another explicit review. The previous publication remains unchanged until replacement. To remove an answer immediately, select its draft in the list and use **Withdraw selected drafts' current published answers**; review history is preserved.

Normal setup reproduces only the three fixed, human-approved releases. It never approves newly imported or edited content. Future regulatory updates require explicit human review. Account registration does not grant staff, draft-edit or publication permissions. Original imported `Source.last_verified_at` values remain empty because their historical metadata was preserved; The owner verification statements and dates belong to the relevant approved frozen releases. Later edits cannot inherit those approvals.
